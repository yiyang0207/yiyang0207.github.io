We present a vision-to-touch framework that generates per-finger tactile signals directly from RGB observations. By augmenting vision-only demonstrations with generated tactile images or latent features, FELT enables visuo-tactile policy learning without requiring physical tactile sensors. Across multiple contact-rich real-world manipulation tasks, it consistently improves performance over vision-only policies.
